Mail CV Google Scholar LinkedIn GitHub

I am currently pursuing an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at KAIST, conducting research on Reasoning and Efficient AI systems at the Scalable and Efficient AI Lab under Prof. Sehoon Kim's supervision. AGI will usher in a new phase of human labor, and what counts as good AI judgment ultimately rests on human values. My work is dedicated to building AGI agents whose behavior aligns with those values.

Previously, I worked with Prof. Taesup Kim at Seoul National University and Prof. Sundong Kim at Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology, researching reasoning and LLM behavior.

I also worked as a visiting researcher at Forschungszentrum Jülich's INM-7 institute, directed by Prof. Simon B. Eickhoff, through the Korea-Germany Junior Research Fellowship, exploring the intersection of neuroscience and machine learning.
