Preprint 2026SEAL, KAIST

ResidualQuant: KV Cache Quantization for Looped Transformers with 2-Bit Residuals

Looped Transformers share weights but not KV caches. ResidualQuant stores one INT4 anchor loop and 2-bit residuals for the rest, recovering BF16 accuracy with 80.7% less KV storage.
